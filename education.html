Education

Discover scholarships and training.

Scholarships

  • Baguio City college education grant: for deserving college students from low-income Baguio families. Usually needs proof of Baguio residency, voter certification and parents' income certificate. Ask at the City Mayor's Office for current requirements.
  • Private and school-based grants: ask your school's scholarship office.

Training and skills

  • TESDA Cordillera (CAR): tech-voc courses, many with free training scholarships. Main campus (CSITE) at Military Cut-Off, Baguio.
  • TESDA Online Program: free online courses you can take at home

Tutoring

Volunteer tutors help learners with reading, math and school requirements. Want to tutor? See Volunteer.
