Emergency?

If someone is in danger, dial 911. In Baguio it goes to the Baguio Smart City Command Center, 24/7.

Baguio hotlines

  • CDRRMO Baguio (disaster and rescue, office concerns): (074) 661-1455 · Globe 0927 628 0498 · Smart 0999 678 4335
  • Police, fire and medical hotlines: see the CDRRMO Baguio contact page
  • Social welfare assistance: DSWD Cordillera (CAR), or visit the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) at City Hall

What we can help with

  • Food and relief goods
  • Medical and medicine assistance referrals
  • Help after typhoons, floods or fire
  • School and learning needs
  • Someone to talk to: see Mental Wellness

How to ask

  • Tell us your name, barangay and how to reach you.
  • Describe what you need and how urgent it is.
  • We'll connect you with the right responder or office.
