Volunteer

Find ways to serve your community.

Ways you can help

  • Relief operations: packing and distributing goods after typhoons and floods
  • Tutoring: helping students with reading, math and homework
  • Clean-up drives: coastal, river and barangay clean-ups
  • Feeding programs: preparing and serving meals for children and seniors
  • Skills sharing: teaching a trade, computer basics or livelihood skills

Volunteer with groups in Baguio

  • Philippine Red Cross: the Baguio Chapter is on Harrison Road. Visit the chapter to sign up for disaster response, first aid and blood services.
  • CDRRMO Baguio: the city's disaster office, for disaster preparedness and response activities

How to get started

  • Choose the kind of work that fits your time and skills.
  • Contact us with your name, barangay and when you're available.
  • We'll match you with the next activity that needs hands.
