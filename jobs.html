Find Work

Job openings, job fairs and free skills training in Baguio.

Where to look for jobs

  • Public Employment Service Office (PESO), Baguio City Hall: free job matching, referrals and local job fairs. Ask at City Hall or watch the Baguio City PIO page for job fair dates.
  • PhilJobNet: the government's free online job board. Search for openings in Baguio and Benguet.
  • DOLE Cordillera on Cabinet Hill, Baguio: labor concerns, employment programs and emergency work (TUPAD). See dole.gov.ph.
  • JobStreet: private job listings. Set the location to Baguio.

Free skills training

  • TESDA Cordillera: free courses and scholarships in trades like automotive, carpentry, cooking and computer skills, with national certificates (NC II).
  • TESDA Online Program: free online courses you can take from your phone.

Avoid job scams

  • Never pay a fee before you get a real job offer.
  • For work abroad, check that the agency is licensed with the Department of Migrant Workers.
  • Be careful with offers that sound too good, ask for your bank details, or only talk through chat apps.
