Support you can find here

  • Someone to listen: talking with a trained peer supporter
  • Referrals to counselors and community health centers
  • Support groups for students, parents and caregivers
  • Simple self-care tips for stress, grief and anxiety

Taking care of yourself

  • Keep a regular sleep and meal routine.
  • Stay connected: reach out to a friend or family member.
  • Move your body, even a short walk helps.
  • It's okay to ask for help. Asking is a strength.
